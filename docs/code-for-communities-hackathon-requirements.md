## 2. Track 05: Track-Based Cyclone Impact & Infrastructure Vulnerability Forecaster

**Theme**: `Resilience`  
**Track**: **Track 05 — Track-Based Cyclone Impact & Infrastructure Vulnerability Forecaster**

### The Problem
> Extreme weather events in the Bay of Bengal and coastal APAC require rapid anticipatory action. Shifting disaster response from post-landfall recovery to pre-landfall evacuation planning, infrastructure hardening, and parametric insurance liquidity saves lives and livelihoods.

### The Challenge
> Build an AI-powered predictive risk and vulnerability modeling platform utilizing Google Earth Engine (GEE) satellite feeds, real-time meteorological data, and Gemini 3.7 Flash's multimodal reasoning. The solution should simulate cyclone storm surges, predict local rainfall damage pathways, map exposure for critical infrastructure (power grids, arterial roads, medical shelters), and automate early-warning advisory dispatches for local municipal and disaster management authorities.

## 3. What to Build & What to Submit

### Mandatory Build Requirements
1. **Functioning End-to-End Flow**: Working prototype for the track's core use case (not just a concept or slide deck).
2. **Mandatory Integration of Google AI**:
   - GenAI / LLM reasoning (Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Vertex AI).
   - Predictive modeling (Vertex AI AutoML, custom models).
   - Vision & multimodal (Gemini Multimodal / Vertex AI Vision for citizen photo analysis, damage reports, flood/hazard monitoring).
   - Language & Voice (Cloud Speech-to-Text, Text-to-Speech, Translation API for linguistic reach across Indian states).
   - Geospatial intelligence (Google Maps Platform, Google Earth Engine, IMD meteorological feeds).
3. **Real or Realistic Data**: Public datasets (e.g. data.gov.in, IMD, ISRO/Bhuvan, municipal/state portals, or simulated live telemetry where APIs are private).
4. **Built for India**: Designed to scale across states, districts, and municipalities — not limited to a single isolated pocket.
5. **Multilingual or Voice Support**: Inclusive access across Indian linguistic regions (Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, etc.).

### Submission Package Checklist
1. **Source Code**: Public or access-granted GitHub repository.
2. **Demo Video**: 3–5 minutes video with an end-to-end working walkthrough.
3. **Pitch Deck**: 10–12 slides covering:
   - Problem statement
   - Solution architecture
   - AI & technical approach (Google AI integration)
   - Target beneficiaries (citizens, administrators, ministries)
   - Deployability & scalability roadmap across India
4. **Brief Description**: 2–3 line executive elevator pitch.
5. **Deployed Link**: Live, functional prototype URL accessible to judges.
